AgriShield photos
=================
FLUTTER APP. Put real photos here to replace the drawn illustrations. Use exactly these
file names (lowercase), ending in .jpg — e.g. home.jpg:

  onboarding  - first screen, full height (portrait works best)
  home        - big card at the top of Home
  storage     - Crop Storage card and page
  scan        - Leaf Scan card (a close-up of a leaf)
  map         - Satellite View card (aerial / satellite view of fields)
  advice      - Farm Advice card (a crop field)

Any name you leave out keeps its drawing. Keep each photo under ~300 KB
(about 1200 px wide) so the app stays fast on slow connections.
Only use photos you took yourself or have the rights to use.
